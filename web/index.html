Cupertab

Cupertab is an experimental WebAssembly port of touchHLE, the high-level emulator for iPhone OS 2.x–4.0 apps. Pick an .ipa you own, or an iPhone OS 4 .ipsw to boot Apple's own SpringBoard from it. To put apps on its home screen, add their .ipa files before booting. Files stay on your machine and run entirely in this tab.

Cupertab ships no Apple firmware and no decryption keys. To boot an .ipsw, enter the root filesystem key for your own firmware. This browser remembers it after a boot.
